---

The credential bar went up as the pool shrank.

By end of 2027, the Early Careers Jobs Alliance will not have published any binding rule on employers to maintain a ratio of junior-to-senior hiring. If that's wrong, tell me what document changed it.

Here's how it looks from 2031. [The DSIT report from October 2026](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ae202189bca21f1c5036e99/artificial-intelligence-adoption-research-report-2026.pdf) names the Early Careers Jobs Alliance: Whitehall, employers and trade unions convened to examine how AI is changing entry-level roles. Twenty million pounds. Practical guidance. Good practice examples. No binding ratios. No floor.

A floor would have cost someone something.

[Adzuna's data show UK entry-level postings fell by roughly a third since ChatGPT launched.](https://www.techuk.org/resource/what-s-actually-happening-with-entry-level-and-graduate-jobs.html) [Entry-level job ads asking for AI skills nearly tripled since the start of 2025.](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-11-05-uk-entry-level-jobs-ai-skills-adzuna-indeed/) Both numbers are in every story this week. Nobody adds them together.

[Indeed's senior economist said the new AI-driven hiring replaced about a third of the junior postings lost elsewhere.](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-11-05-uk-entry-level-jobs-ai-skills-adzuna-indeed/) Start at 100 posts. Lose 32. A third of those come back: about 11. You end at 79. Roughly one in five junior posts is gone. The tripling of AI-skill ads is real. It sits on top of a market that is a fifth smaller.

A job listing from 2022 needed a degree. The same listing in 2026 needs a degree, ChatGPT fluency, and [sometimes the kind of strategic thinking that used to take five years to grow.](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/1/services/ai/ai-jobs-barometer.html) The job didn't get promoted. The queue to reach it did.

[Work Foundation research found 36% of UK employers had cut entry-level roles for 16 to 24-year-olds over the previous year, with 43% citing AI and money spent on machines as the reason.](https://perplexityaimagazine.com/expert-insights/ai-jobs-uk-2026/) [Most of the new AI-skill postings are old jobs in sales, software and admin with a new line added.](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-11-05-uk-entry-level-jobs-ai-skills-adzuna-indeed/) The doors didn't multiply. They got pickier.

The graduates of 2026 had two problems at once. Fewer places to start. A higher bar to clear them. The advice was: get the new skills. The advice was not wrong. The building it pointed at had got smaller.

The employers who cut the junior roles are on the alliance. They're being asked to share good practice. They are not being asked to replace what they removed. That's a floor problem. There is no floor.
