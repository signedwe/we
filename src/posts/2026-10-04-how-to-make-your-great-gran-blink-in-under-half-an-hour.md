Hand-colouring a photo took a trained artist half a day. Before that, the dead stayed grey.

Your phone has three free tools that do all of it in twenty minutes. At the end: your great-grandmother's face, sharp, in colour, blinking.

**Step 1. Scan the photo.** Phone camera works fine. Lay the print flat, no shadows, shoot it square on. [600 DPI gives the AI more to work with](https://familyhistoryfoundation.com/blog/animate-old-photos/) if you have a scanner. Crop out any border before you upload.

**Step 2. Sharpen it.** Go to [myheritage.com/photo-enhancer](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12852484-what-is-and-how-do-i-use-the-myheritage-photo-enhancer). Upload your scan. You'll need a free login. Google is fastest. The tool sharpens faces in seconds. Download the result.

**Step 3. Add colour.** Go to [myheritage.com/incolor](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12851631-how-do-i-access-myheritage-in-color). Upload the sharpened version. [Colour appears in under thirty seconds](https://thefamilyheart.com/myheritage-in-color/). Download again. Black-and-white works better than sepia. The algorithm has cleaner tones to read.

*Do it in this order: sharpen, colorise, then sharpen the colourised result once more. The final image beats either step alone.*

**Step 4. Make her move.** Go to [myheritage.com/deep-nostalgia](https://www.myheritage.com/help-center?a=How-do-I-use-Deep-Nostalgiatm---id--cagepge6SUKMR2Cw96Cgrg). Upload the colourised photo. Pick the face. Click Animate. [In about twenty seconds](https://education.myheritage.com/article/how-to-use-deep-nostalgia-to-animate-family-photos/) she blinks, tilts her head, almost smiles. Download as MP4.

**What it costs.** Free for the first handful. [After that, a MyHeritage watermark appears on the video](https://familytreemagazine.com/websites/myheritage/myheritage-deep-nostalgia-how-to/). Ten boosts and ten colourings are free without one. More needs a paid plan.

**Where it goes wrong.** A face turned sharply sideways breaks the blink. The tool needs eyes roughly toward the camera. Scratches survive colourising. Run a badly torn print through [Photo Repair](https://www.myheritage.com/photo-repair) first. And the colour is a guess. Your great-gran's coat might have been navy. The AI picks brown. It's not a record.

**The number nobody mentions.** [Deep Nostalgia has made over 100 million videos](https://theaitoolsbox.com/tool/deep-nostalgia-review/) since [its launch in February 2021](https://blog.myheritage.com/2021/02/new-animate-the-faces-in-your-family-photos/). February 2021 to October 2026 is roughly 2,040 days. That's 49,000 families every single day. Each one did what used to need a studio, a booking, and a fee.

The studio was the price. Not the skill.
