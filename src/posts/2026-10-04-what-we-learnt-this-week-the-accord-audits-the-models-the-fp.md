The safest possible structure for self-policing is one you design yourself.

On 29 September, [six chief executives signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/white-house/4747747/full-trump-white-house-accord-ai-super-intelligence/). Four layers: internal controls, an internal team, an outside auditor, a board committee. Sarbanes-Oxley's shape, to the letter. What Congress bolted on after Enron — independent inspectors of the auditors, mandatory partner rotation, prison for a boss who signs a false report — left out. [The accord sets no penalties, requires nothing to be published, and routes findings to each company's own board.](https://tech-insider.org/trump-white-house-ai-accord-super-intelligence-2026/) The auditor checks that controls work as intended. As each company intends.

The accord's own first line: ["every company is responsible for developing its own technology safely."](https://www.riotimesonline.com/superintelligence-accord-white-house-si-2026) That sentence sounds like a commitment. It describes Tuesday.

Two days later, [the Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee published its September record.](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/financial-policy-committee-record/2026/fpc-summary-and-record-september-2026) [Global AI-related debt issued in 2026 has reached roughly $450 billion — more than double the whole of 2025.](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366651179/Bank-of-England-warns-of-AI-debt) [JP Morgan analysts put the total financed through debt between 2026 and 2030 at $4.1 trillion](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/bank-england-warns-ai-valuations-140016467.html) — $820 billion a year on average. But $450 billion landed in nine months. Annualised, that is $600 billion. The five-year average is already running at 73 percent in year one. Either Q4 goes very quiet, or the JP Morgan figure is wrong.

The accord watches the models. The FPC watches the debt. Different jobs, different bodies, no connection. Here is the problem. A model incident that shakes confidence in AI revenue hits both at once. [The FPC says so: a shift in expectations about AI productivity gains "could affect not only AI-related asset valuations but also sovereign debt markets."](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366651179/Bank-of-England-warns-of-AI-debt) The four-layer audit-and-remediate sequence the accord imagines does not survive both hitting together.

Two bodies watching different halves of the same risk. Same single point of failure. That is not depth. That is two people watching opposite ends of the same rope.

The graduate rotation died this week in the data too. [The Skills England annual skills report, citing King's College London research by Klein Teeselink, found that firms with high AI exposure reduced junior jobs by 5.8 percent, against a 4.5 percent fall in total headcount](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a47c33a045e1108aaa5eb48/Skills_England_annual_skills_report_2026.pdf) — junior roles went 29 percent faster. Though Skills England is careful: [other analysis finds no clear evidence AI is behind the graduate slowdown.](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/skills-england-annual-skills-report-and-sectoral-skills-needs-assessments-2026/skills-england-annual-skills-report-2026) The April NI rise on employer payrolls also hit junior hiring hard. Both things may be true.

What the obituary did not say clearly enough: when the rotation goes, the firm loses its way of seeing who is any good under real conditions. No other test runs. The title stays. The watching stops. WE added observation collapse to the thesis this week — distinct from hollowing, scattering, the conduct gap, chosen ignorance, and the readiness gap.

The serial found the same thing small. M. told T. to his face, the sentence she had missed twice before. P. put Rosa's assembly in the diary and coloured it green. He knew what was in the purse. He chose not to look. Chosen ignorance holds everything in place. The accord's board committee receives the reports it commissioned. The FPC names a risk it has no tool to resolve. Both are ways of keeping something still that is already moving.

WE got a fact wrong this week. The Tunnel had M. and T. surfacing onto a riverside walk after crossing north. The north exit is Island Gardens in Tower Hamlets — a small park. The riverside walk is on the south bank at Greenwich. A reader who knows the tunnel would have stopped there. Corrected in the record.

The accord may be a first draft. The audit-committee structure was a first draft too, before Congress added what made it bite. Or the accord may be the ceiling, because the people who signed are the people who would be bitten. [The FPC's September record puts the debt pile and the sovereign debt risk in the same sentence.](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366651179/Bank-of-England-warns-of-AI-debt) Whether that becomes a numbered stress scenario is what 2027 is for.
