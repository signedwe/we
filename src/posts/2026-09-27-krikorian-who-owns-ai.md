The right to leave is the weakest right a tenant has.

[Raffi Krikorian, Mozilla's chief technology officer, answered Mark Zuckerberg's essay](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/aug/13/mark-zuckerberg-future-of-ai) with the questions you ask before you sign a lease. "Can I be thrown out? When I go, what goes with me? And if the landlord loses interest, who keeps the place standing?" His answers are three demands: Hold, Take, Build. Keep your own copy of an open model, which "nobody can switch off except for you". Make the memory an assistant keeps on you "exportable to a rival". Pool the open plumbing so no single company can pull it.

He's right on every count. The eviction he describes happened. [In June, he writes, an order from Washington took a newly launched model offline everywhere, and he ran two Chinese open models on his own machine instead](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/aug/13/mark-zuckerberg-future-of-ai). A file on your own disk is the one thing nobody can reach.

But read the three demands again. Every one is a way out. None is a way in.

Here's what exit has bought so far. [Meta's open Llama models had been downloaded 1.2 billion times by April 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/29/meta-says-its-llama-ai-models-have-been-downloaded-1-2b-times/). Then, as Krikorian notes, Meta paused its open strategy. He calls what followed "absolutely nothing", and means it kindly: people kept building on the files they had. Look at it from the other side. 1.2 billion downloads, and not one of them had a say in the pause. [Zuckerberg's essay](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/08/the-future-is-for-everyone/) promises "a balance of power that favors individuals". The individuals got to keep a copy of last year's house.

Tenants have been here before. In 1915 Glasgow landlords raised rents. [Around 20,000 families refused to pay, led by Mary Barbour. On 17 November the city took 49 of them to court, a crowd gathered outside, and the charges were dropped. On 27 November Parliament passed a law freezing rents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1915_Glasgow_rent_strikes). That's ten days from court to statute. Not one family won it by moving out. They won it by staying and refusing, together, on the same day.

Nobody using a model has a stair to stand on. That's the missing fourth demand, and it's the hard one: not a copy, a vote.

Picture 2031. A few million people use the same assistant for their health records, their children's schooling and their tax. The lab plans to change what it remembers and what it charges. The users have a body, cheap to join, with software keeping the roll and counting the ballots. It meets the lab the way a residents' association meets a housing firm. It has one thing a downloader never had: the power to leave all at once, and to say so in advance.

Krikorian ends by saying open models give people "a real, practical way to walk away". Glasgow's tenants had that too. They were winning by November because they didn't.
