The agreement looks fine. It always looks fine. That's the whole problem.

[Since 1 May 2026](https://www.gm-services.co.uk/blog/written-statement-of-terms), England's private rental rules changed completely. The Renters' Rights Act 2025 abolished fixed terms, no-fault evictions and blanket no-pet bans. [Most letting agents are still sending out the old template.](https://www.gm-services.co.uk/blog/written-statement-of-terms) Nothing pinged to say the PDF on their desktop was partly unlawful. The document looks respectable. That's what makes it dangerous.

At the end of this you'll know which clauses in your agreement have no legal force. Twenty minutes. Costs nothing.

**What you're hunting.** [Clauses that are now void](https://www.remedylegal.ai/blog/unfair-tenancy-agreement-clauses-uk-unenforceable) include: anything about Section 21 or fixed-term eviction; compulsory professional cleaning at the end of the tenancy; administration, referencing or inventory fees; a right of entry without reasonable notice. [An illegal clause doesn't disappear from the agreement.](https://flatcheckuk.co.uk/guides/tenancy-agreement-red-flags) It sits there waiting. It stops binding you only when you notice it.

**Step 1.** Get your agreement as a PDF. The agent probably emailed it, so it's already one. Paper copy? Photograph each page. [Adobe Scan](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/mobile/scanner-app.html) turns those photos into a single readable file in two minutes.

**Step 2.** Open [Claude](https://claude.ai) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com). No paid account needed for this. Click the paperclip. Upload the PDF. [Claude reads the whole document as context, scanned pages included.](https://claudable.work/blog/can-claude-read-pdfs)

**Step 3.** Paste this prompt exactly:

*I am a tenant in England. This is my tenancy agreement. Flag any clause that may be unenforceable or illegal under the Tenant Fees Act 2019, the Consumer Rights Act 2015, or the Renters' Rights Act 2025. Include any mention of Section 21, fixed terms, compulsory fees or unlimited landlord entry. For each one, quote the clause and say in one plain sentence what the problem is.*

**Step 4.** Take every flagged clause back to the original page. [AI maps clauses against current legislation](https://www.remedylegal.ai/blog/ai-tenant-rights-checker-uk-how-it-works) but can misread a scanned word. The page number is in the response. Go there.

**Step 5.** Found something? Ask the agent to remove it before you sign. Most will. The clause went in by accident. The fix takes thirty seconds.

**Cost.** Zero on the unpaid tier of either assistant.

**Where it goes wrong.** Scanned agreements sometimes produce a misread word. The prompt covers England only. Scotland and Wales have different housing rules. [The Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies across the UK](https://www.kaeltripton.com/before-you/sign-a-tenancy-agreement/) for unfair terms, but housing protections vary by nation.

**A specialist option.** [FlatCheck](https://flatcheckuk.co.uk) and [RenterCheck](https://rentercheck.co.uk/tenancy-agreement-checker) are built for UK tenancies, trained on current housing law, and produce a formatted report you can send to the agent. Both run on Claude. Worth paying for if you want something in writing.

If you signed before May 2026, the illegal clauses are still void. The agent just doesn't know you know.
