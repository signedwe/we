Every musician alive has a voice memo graveyard. Thirty seconds of humming, recorded at 11pm, labelled "tune idea" and never opened again.

You now have enough to do something about it. [Suno](https://suno.com) turns a text description, or an uploaded clip of you humming, into a full song with vocals, production, and a proper arrangement, [in under a minute](https://www.rapidevelopers.com/blog/what-is-suno-ai-everything-you-need-to-know). No studio. No producer. No piano lessons.

By the end of this, you'll have a playable, downloadable track.

Here's what it used to cost. A session musician runs around £300 for a half-day. [Suno's free plan gives you 10 songs a day](https://suno.bi/en/blog/suno-ai-free-what-you-get), no credit card. Over a year, that's 3,650 songs. At studio rates, that's a bill of just over a million pounds. The tool is free.

**Step 1: Make an account at [suno.com](https://suno.com).** Free. No card.

**Step 2: Go to Create.** You'll see a prompt box. Type something like: *"upbeat indie pop, female vocals, acoustic guitar, about missing a city you grew up in."* [Plain language works fine.](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/how-to-use-suno) Suno reads genre, mood, and topic from what you write.

*Copy this into the prompt box to try it: upbeat indie pop, female vocals, acoustic guitar, nostalgic, about coming home*

**Step 3: Hit Create.** [Two versions appear.](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/how-to-use-suno) Suno runs the same prompt down two different creative paths so you can pick. Each costs 5 credits.

**Step 4: Have a melody? Upload it.** Got a voice memo of you humming the chorus at 11pm? [The free tier lets you upload up to 8 minutes of audio.](https://musicmake.ai/blog/suno-ai-free-plan-2026) Suno uses it as a melodic seed and builds the arrangement around your tune.

**Step 5: Extend or fix a section.** Love the verse, hate the chorus? Don't start again. [Click the three-dot menu on your track, choose Remix/Edit, then Extend.](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-extend) Set a start timestamp, describe what you want next, and Suno continues from that point. The mood and key carry over.

**Step 6: Download.** Free tracks download as MP3. [They are not cleared for commercial use on the free plan.](https://musicmake.ai/blog/suno-ai-free-plan-2026) For personal listening, sharing with friends, or figuring out whether the idea is any good: fine.

**What goes wrong.** The first generation is almost never perfect. Treat it like a sketch. Suno can produce lyrics that sound right but mean nothing. If the vocal is flat, add "emotional" or "soulful" to the prompt. If it sounds like hotel lobby music, be specific: "verse/chorus structure, loud pre-chorus, drop." Your hum is a suggestion, not a contract. The melody may get bent.

**How long it takes.** Account and first song: ten minutes. Getting something you actually like: about an hour of experimenting.
