You've heard it. Something beautiful from a hedge or a high branch. You looked up, saw nothing, walked on.

Now you can stop and know. Thirty seconds, no fee.

The [Merlin Bird ID app](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/sound-id/) from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology is free. No subscription. [No credit card](https://support.ebird.org/en/support/solutions/articles/48000961587-merlin-bird-id-faqs). Its Sound ID feature listens to the birds around you and shows each species' name in real time, even when several are singing at once. [It covers over 2,000 species by sound](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/sound-id/), with comprehensive coverage across Europe, Britain and Ireland included.

Here's a number from nowhere in the sources. The [Britain and Ireland pack](https://ebird.org/news/merlin-bird-id-launches-for-the-uk-and-ireland) lists 400+ species. [Sound ID covers 2,066 species](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/sound-id/) without needing any pack downloaded. That's five times the range of the standard UK pack, in your pocket, free. The robin outside your kitchen this morning was already trackable by a computer. You just didn't have the app.

Knowing birdsong took years. You either grew up with someone who knew the calls or you were lost. A twitcher friend could identify a siskin from fifty metres. You could stand beside them for a decade and still not catch up. That knowledge sat inside people and didn't move.

**How to start, right now:**

1. Download [Merlin Bird ID by Cornell Lab](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/) — free on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/merlin-bird-id-by-cornell-lab/id773457673) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.labs.merlinbirdid.app&hl=en_US). Two minutes.
2. Create a free Cornell Lab account. You can skip this for five days, but do it. It saves your life list.
3. Download the **Britain and Ireland pack** over wifi. This gives you photos and recordings to match what you hear.
4. Tap **Sound ID**. Hold your phone up. Don't move.
5. Watch names appear in real time. Tap any name to see the bird, hear a comparison recording, find out where it nests.

*Try this on a still morning before traffic starts: open Sound ID at your front door and count how many species appear in ten minutes without moving an inch.*

**Where it goes wrong:** Merlin gives occasional wrong answers. Wind, traffic, a lawnmower two gardens over — all can push it off course. [Birdforum users note](https://www.birdforum.net/threads/did-merlin-bird-id-really-hear-these-in-my-garden.441703/) it sometimes flags species very unlikely to be present. Early morning, quiet spot, that's when it's best. Red and orange dots next to a name mean rare or uncommon for your location. Treat those as a reason to double-check. Not a discovery.

**What it costs:** Nothing. **How long:** Under five minutes from download to your first named bird. **Works offline:** Yes, once the pack is downloaded.

The thing this replaces wasn't a fee. It was a relationship. A parent, a grandparent, a patient friend who knew what things were called and was willing to stand still long enough to show you. Not everyone had that person. Now the park is readable by anyone with a phone, on equal terms.
