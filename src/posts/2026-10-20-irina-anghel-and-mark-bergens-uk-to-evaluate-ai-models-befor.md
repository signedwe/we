Here is what nobody said when the access came back: the freeze proved the arrangement fragile, not strong.

It's 25 September 2026. An evaluator at the AI Security Institute has a desk in Whitehall, a government laptop, and testing software open. [The previous day, the White House asked OpenAI and Anthropic to stop sharing new models with British testers](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-restores-uk-access-ai-model-testing/) until Washington finished its own review. Nobody rang London first. The software just ran empty.

For seventeen days, the UK's main tool for checking frontier AI before release had nothing to check. Not because the models weren't ready. Because a foreign government said so.

[The access came back on 11 October](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/uk-reverses-trump-plans-to-keep-back-openai-anthropic-models). By end of 2027, it will be cut again. That's the prediction, and here's why it costs something: the Institute has no legal power to compel a safety test from any lab, British or American. [The House of Commons Library said so plainly last week](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10003/): no AI-specific law, no single AI regulator. What Britain has is agreements. Voluntary ones. The kind a White House memo can pause before breakfast.

AI Minister Kanishka Narayan said Britain had been "working with the US government to retain access." Working. That word is doing a lot. You work to keep something when you don't have the right to it.

The counter-argument: seventeen days is short. Lobbying worked. Isn't that durability?

No. A house is not durable because it survived one storm if someone else holds the keys. The Institute didn't fight off the freeze. It waited for the freeze to lift. Those are different things, and the coverage mostly missed it.

[During the pause, Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5.1 was reportedly kept from the Institute's bench](https://cryptobriefing.com/uk-ai-security-institute-regains-model-access/). Whether it has since been tested is an early signal of how fully things returned to normal. No one has published that answer.

The evaluator's screen is live again. A model is arriving. Everything is working, because the phone call went well this time.
