Sam has a dial meter. Five little clock faces in a row on the wall under the stairs. His supplier sends an estimated bill every quarter. He pays it. He has no idea if it's right.

By the end of this, you'll have the actual reading, in the right format, ready to paste into your supplier's portal. Four minutes.

**Why bother?**

If you're on a standard variable tariff without a smart meter, [your supplier estimates your usage](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/utilities/meter-reading-day---what-you-need-to-know/) when you don't submit a reading. An estimate is the supplier's guess, not yours. The energy price cap [rose 3.6% on 1 October 2025](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/utilities/meter-reading-day---what-you-need-to-know/), so an overestimate now costs more than it did. A real reading fixes that immediately.

Dial meters are especially bad. The hands run in alternating directions and you have to know which way each one goes. People get them wrong constantly.

**What YouTube can't do**

YouTube can show you how a dial meter works in general. It can't look at *your* meter, in *your* under-stairs cupboard, and tell you what it says right now. [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) or [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) can.

**What you need**

- Your phone
- A ChatGPT or Gemini account. Both are free to start.
- Two minutes of decent light near your meter

**The steps**

1. Find your meter. Gas and electricity are usually separate boxes. Take a clear, well-lit photo of the display. For dial meters, get close enough that each clock face fills most of the frame.

2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini on your phone.

3. Upload the photo. Then paste this:

*This is a photo of my [gas / electricity] meter. Please read the digits carefully, left to right, and ignore any red digits or digits after a decimal point. Tell me the reading I should submit to my energy supplier.*

4. It'll give you the number. For a dial meter, also ask: *Please describe which way each dial hand is pointing, and give me the reading digit by digit, explaining your reasoning for any dial that looks ambiguous.*

5. Cross-check it yourself. Glance at the meter. If the number looks obviously wrong, trust your eyes and ask again.

6. [Submit the reading to your supplier](https://www.energybrokers.co.uk/news/suppliers/how-to-submit-your-meter-readings-to-all-suppliers) via their app, website or phone. Most have a portal that takes under a minute.

**Where it goes wrong**

Glare off the meter glass is the main failure. Take the photo at a slight angle rather than straight on. If a dial hand sits exactly on a number rather than between two, the AI sometimes gets the direction of travel wrong. Check that one by eye. Economy 7 meters show two separate rates: press the button to cycle through both displays and photograph each one separately.

**One thing the post can't fix**

If your meter is in a shared cupboard in a block of flats and the lock has been changed, [you're entitled to access](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/information-for-household-consumers/meters). A lot of people aren't getting it. The AI reads what you can photograph. If you can't photograph it, you're still stuck.

**What it costs**

Nothing. Both ChatGPT and Gemini read uploaded images without a paid account.
