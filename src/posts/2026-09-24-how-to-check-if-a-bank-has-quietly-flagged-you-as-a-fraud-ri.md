A fraud label can close your bank account without a court, a charge, or a letter.

It's called a CIFAS marker. Banks file them. So do insurers, telecoms companies, and civil service employers. [One can follow you for six years.](https://www.cifas.org.uk/dsar) You won't be told it exists. The usual discovery is a refused loan, a closed account, a mortgage that went quiet.

You have the right to see yours. Free. Within a month.

**What you'll have at the end:** your CIFAS file, and a draft dispute letter if something's there.

**Time:** under an hour. No cost.

**One thing to know first.** [Disputes succeed roughly 20 to 30 percent of the time.](https://marketinvoice.co.uk/guides/cifas-impact/) Worth doing anyway. You can't argue with what you can't see.

---

**Step 1. Get two documents ready.**

A passport or driving licence. A bank statement or utility bill no older than ninety days, showing your current address. Have both as PDFs or clear phone photos.

**Step 2. Submit the free request.**

Go to [cifas.org.uk/dsar](https://www.cifas.org.uk/dsar) and fill in the form. Give every name you've used, your date of birth, and every address from the last six years.

*That last part matters more than anything. [An incomplete address history is the most common reason a request comes back blank when a marker does exist.](https://www.clearmyname.uk/blog/how-to-check-if-you-have-a-cifas-marker) The record matches on name, date of birth, and address together. Miss one postcode and it won't surface.*

CIFAS must reply within one calendar month. The clock starts when they have enough to identify you, not when you first make contact.

**Step 3. Read the report.**

Nothing there? You're clear. If a marker exists, the report names the category — False Application, Misuse of Facility — and the organisation that filed it. [It won't show you the evidence they used.](https://cifasmarker.org.uk/guides/making-a-dsar-for-cifas/) That's the next job.

**Step 4. Use an AI tool to draft the complaint.**

Open Claude (claude.ai, free) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, free tier). Give it the marker category and the name of the filing organisation. Try:

*Draft a formal complaint to [organisation] about a CIFAS marker filed under [category]. My name is [name], date of birth [DOB]. I believe this was filed in error because [your reason]. Ask them to show the evidence and remove the record if they can't stand it up. Tone: clear, factual, firm.*

Edit what comes back. The tool removes the blank-page problem. You supply the facts.

**Step 5. Complain to the filing organisation.**

[CIFAS can't remove a marker until the organisation that filed it agrees to.](https://www.cifas.org.uk/contact-us/i-want-to-make-a-complaint) Go through their complaints process. Keep every reply.

If they say no: the Financial Ombudsman (for banks and insurers) or the ICO (for data accuracy complaints). Both are free.

**The move most guides skip.** Send a separate data request to the filing organisation, asking for everything they hold on the account or application in question. The CIFAS report shows the marker. Their own file shows what it's based on. You need both to build a real challenge.

---

**Where it goes wrong.**

A well-documented marker won't shift on a letter alone. The challenge has to engage with the specific evidence. That's why the second request matters.

[Don't pay anyone to do this for you.](https://www.cifas.org.uk/dsar) The right is yours. The form is free.
