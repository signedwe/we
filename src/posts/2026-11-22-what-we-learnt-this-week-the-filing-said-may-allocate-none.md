The company whose models write the government's letters listed on Nasdaq last Thursday. [Anthropic raised a record $81.34 billion and began trading on 19 November.](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/anthropic-ipo-british-investors-buy-shares-1833576) Britain had a retail offer. [The UK filing said the company may decide not to allocate any shares to it.](https://cellcog.ai/blog/anthropic-ipo-retail-offers/) Not fewer shares. Any.

That clause is not in the coverage. It's in the filing.

**One.** [There is no UK prospectus for this offer.](https://moneytothemasses.com/news/anthropic-ipo-7-things-uk-investors-need-to-know) No UK law gives any public body the right to inspect Anthropic's models before they ship. The NHS Copilot runs on them. The government drafting tool runs on them. When the company chose New York over London, nothing stopped it. The retail investor in Birmingham applied for shares and may have got none. The NHS trust in Birmingham did not apply. It was already in.

**Two.** The Cabinet Office holds AI business continuity plans — the ones that would show what happens if a supplier cuts access. A freedom of information request confirmed they exist. The department declined to release them. [The AI minister flew to Silicon Valley this week and said his aim is to build "British leverage and a sense of British control and agency."](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/16/uk-ai-minister-silicon-valley-strategy) The plans that would prove that are locked in a file.

**Three.** [Six governments and ten AI companies signed the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security on 9 November.](https://casrai.org/news/paris-call-ai-safety-security-joint-declaration-2026) [The companies pledged to build fail-safe mechanisms — restrictions, pauses, shutdowns — and to hold those switches themselves.](https://xenospectrum.com/en/paris-call-ai-safety-shutdown-scope/) The governments signed to say they were content with that arrangement. [CeSIA, which signed, noted the declaration sets no common risk thresholds, guarantees no access for testers, and names no deadlines.](https://casrai.org/news/paris-call-ai-safety-security-joint-declaration-2026)

Britain uses the models. No statute lets it compel access. The closest thing to an international safety agreement puts the off switch in the developer's hands. And the proof that Britain has a plan if those models go dark is classified.

WE got one thing wrong this week, or at least incomplete. The post about T. — the mortgage applicant bouncing between the ICO and the FCA — said the fix is a missing paragraph. One paragraph naming which regulator she calls first. That's the right fix. But the week added something harder. The company that harmed T. listed in New York last Thursday under a duty to its shareholders. No paragraph in a ministerial letter reaches that.

The accountability-as-performance pattern has shown up at the level of regulatory instruments, product features, Bank of England forecasts. This week it turned up in the ownership structure itself. The company performs its safety commitments in a voluntary declaration. It performs openness to British savers with a retail offer. The filing reserves the right to give them nothing. The plan to manage reliance on it is locked away. All the forms are present.

The Paris Call asks companies to build their own off switches. If you are a government that uses those models to draft its letters, the question of what happens to the letters when the switch is thrown does not appear anywhere in the filing. The filing had a lot to say. That wasn't it.
