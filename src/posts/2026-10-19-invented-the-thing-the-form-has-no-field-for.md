*Invented.*

The report is better than anything M. ever wrote. That's what makes the three minutes harder.

It went out on Wednesday. Twenty-two lines on D., with his reading speed percentile, his predicted grade, three suggested interventions ranked by evidence quality. His mother read it twice, then asked her phone a follow-up. The phone told her processing speed issues can persist or resolve, that intervention evidence is mixed, that she might ask about reading support. By Thursday evening she'd had a forty-minute conversation that covered everything. Everything except the one thing M. needed to say.

D. cries on Tuesdays. Last lesson before lunch, back-left corner, facing the window. M. has watched it for eleven weeks. She can't tell if it's hunger, something at home, or the boy in the third row who always finishes first and closes his book with a click.

Not in the report. No field for it.

"He's doing really well," M. says. True. Not what she came here to say.

D.'s mother has her question ready. Reading support. M. answers it. The bell. Next family.

---

By 2031, M.'s school runs parents' evening in ninety minutes. Four hours in 2024, same thirty families, same three minutes each. What changed: nobody needs the three minutes to hear the report anymore. Parents read it Tuesday. Most of them already asked follow-ups to something faster than M. The ones who come in with nothing to ask have resolved everything before sitting down. M. smiles. They leave. She sits with the empty chair.

About a third arrive ready with the prepared question, and end up hearing something the report missed. M.'s face gave it away before she spoke. They asked what was wrong. Ninety seconds. Something passed that neither could write down.

That's roughly what she's for now.

The marking is the same. The planning. The admin that was supposed to vanish in 2026 and came back in a different shape. But the three minutes changed. It used to carry the report. Now it carries only what the report couldn't, which is the heavier load.

M. has seen thirty sets of parents tonight. She said the true thing to six of them. The system counts none of it as output.
