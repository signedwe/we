Mia spent twenty minutes on Saturday designing a coat of arms on her phone. Fox. Wave. The word DOGGED in the motto banner. It's now on a mug.

By the end of this afternoon, you'll have a design you can print, frame, put on a card, or order on a mug.

[As of January 2026, the College of Arms charges £9,600](https://www.college-of-arms.gov.uk/services/granting-arms) for a personal grant of arms and crest. Eligibility takes into account [awards from the Crown, military commissions, university degrees, and eminence in national or local life](https://www.college-of-arms.gov.uk/services/granting-arms). [The College has been running this since its incorporation in 1484.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Arms) Five hundred and forty-two years of deciding who deserves a lion.

ChatGPT Plus costs £20 a month. Divide £9,600 by £20: that's 480 months, or forty years of subscriptions, for one vellum scroll. Mia used the free tier.

**Work out what you stand for.** Not what sounds noble. [List two or three values, a place, a profession, a family tradition, a meaningful plant or animal, and an optional motto.](https://mew.design/create/ai-family-crest-generator) Your city. Your dog. The sea your grandmother crossed. These become the charges — the symbols on the shield.

**Learn one rule.** The Rule of Tincture: [never place a metal — gold or silver — on a metal, or a colour on a colour.](https://www.aiphotogenerator.net/blog/2026/02/ai-coat-of-arms-generator-guide) Put it in your prompt. It's the gap between heraldry and a gaming clan badge.

**Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini** on the free tier, or try [getimg.ai](https://getimg.ai/use-cases/coat-of-arms) which is free and purpose-built. Type something like:

*"Traditional heraldic coat of arms. Shield divided per pale, azure and gold. A silver fox on the left side, a blue wave on the right. Motto banner reading 'DOGGED.' Perfect bilateral symmetry. Medieval heraldic style. Follow the Rule of Tincture."*

[Generate four to eight variations and pick the best elements to refine in your next prompt.](https://www.aiphotogenerator.net/blog/2026/02/ai-coat-of-arms-generator-guide) [The most common mistake is too many elements: real heraldry is about clarity. A shield needs to be identifiable from across a battlefield. Two or three elements, and let them breathe.](https://www.aiphotogenerator.net/blog/2026/02/ai-coat-of-arms-generator-guide)

**Costs:** Free at getimg.ai. £20/month for ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro.

**Time:** Twenty minutes, plus ten for the motto argument.

**Where it goes wrong:** [Coats of arms are inherently symmetrical. If your output is lopsided, specify "perfect bilateral symmetry" and regenerate.](https://www.aiphotogenerator.net/blog/2026/02/ai-coat-of-arms-generator-guide) AI garbles text in motto banners. Write yours separately and paste it on in Canva or your phone's photo editor. [If you understand what the symbols, colours and layouts actually mean, you can prompt any AI to create something that looks authentic rather than like a fantasy game loading screen.](https://www.aiphotogenerator.net/blog/2026/02/ai-coat-of-arms-generator-guide)

**What this isn't:** A legal grant of arms. The College's vellum scroll, the inherited arms your descendants can quarter — that's still £9,600, still Queen Victoria Street, still the petition. If you want arms that exist in English law, the old price stands.

If you want something beautiful on the wall by Sunday: Mia got there on a free account.
