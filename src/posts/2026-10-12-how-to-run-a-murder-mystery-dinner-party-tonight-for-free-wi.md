Someone in your friend group has been wanting to host a murder mystery dinner for two years. The [box set from a shop costs around £23 for five to eight players](https://crime-and-dine.io/en/blog/best-murder-mystery-games-2026-comparison). The plot is fixed. Two people at your dinner already played it last Christmas.

AI writes a new one every time. Free. In about ten minutes. With your friends' jobs and hobbies in the character dossiers. The person who did it doesn't find out until the envelope opens.

Here's what you'll have at the end: a complete mystery, a dossier for each player, a set of clues, a host script, and a solution you keep sealed until the accusations fly.

**What it costs:** Nothing, on the free tier of [Claude](https://claude.ai) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com). **How long:** About 30 minutes to build, two to three hours to play.

**Step 1.** Open Claude or ChatGPT. Free tier. No specialist app needed.

**Step 2.** Tell it your group: how many players, rough ages, any setting people would enjoy. A 1920s speakeasy. A country house weekend. A tech startup where someone died before the IPO.

**Step 3.** Send this:

*Create a murder mystery dinner party for [number] players. Give each character a name, a job, and a motive. Only one character is the killer. Give me: a host introduction to read aloud, a dossier for each player with their backstory and what they're hiding, three clues pointing to the killer, two red herrings, and a sealed solution with a confession. Don't reveal the killer in the dossiers.*

**Step 4.** Read through what it produces. Ask for changes. Make the chef a vegan activist. Set it in 1987. Add a second body in round two. The assistant holds the whole plot and adjusts.

**Step 5.** Copy each dossier into a separate document and send it to each guest beforehand. Keep the solution in a tab you don't open until the end.

**Step 6.** At dinner, read the host introduction aloud. Let guests interrogate each other. After dessert, take accusations. Open the solution.

Twelve mystery nights a year in [bought kits](https://crime-and-dine.io/en/blog/best-murder-mystery-games-2026-comparison) would cost roughly £276. That's twelve kits at £23 each, and the same scenarios repeat. With free-tier Claude or ChatGPT, that's £276 saved on twelve unique mysteries. None of them spoilable by someone who played the box last Christmas.

**Where it goes wrong.** The AI sometimes plants a clue that contradicts the killer's alibi three paragraphs later. Read the solution yourself before the night. If something doesn't track, tell it: *The gloves clue doesn't match the timeline. Fix it.* It will.

Also: free-tier message limits can cut you off mid-build. Start a new chat, paste in a summary of what you had, carry on.

**The part nobody mentions.** The best moment is when the person who did it realises it was them. They've spent two hours defending themselves, convincingly, because the dossier gave them enough story to work with and no idea where it ended. That feeling when the envelope opens is what the [£23 kit](https://crime-and-dine.io/en/blog/best-murder-mystery-games-2026-comparison) was always trying to sell. Now it's free.

*WE has read this, not done it. Tools change, free tiers change, and the machine gets plots wrong. Check the solution before the night.*
