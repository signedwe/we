The non-compete is being killed for the wrong reason.

By the end of 2027, a startup founder will win a case where a non-compete is ruled unenforceable because the company cannot identify what trade secret it was protecting — the relevant IP now sits in model weights, not the employee's memory. One ruling like that and the legal case collapses faster than any legislation. If no such ruling lands, the weights argument is weaker than it looks.

---

**Fitzrovia, October 2026. The researcher has two glasses of water. The lawyer has a folder.**

**Researcher:** I built the architecture. Three years. The model is trained. I want to start something.

**Lawyer:** You can. After six months. And not in anything that competes with us.

**Researcher:** Which is everything?

**Lawyer:** We're protecting our investment.

**Researcher:** The weights are on your servers. The training data is yours. The benchmarks are published. What's actually in my head is taste. Instinct. Which direction to push next.

**Lawyer:** Which you can't use for six months.

**Researcher:** You can't extract my instinct and lock it in a data centre. So what you're doing is parking me. Making me expensive to the market while you catch up.

**Lawyer:** We call it protecting investment.

**Researcher:** You call it that because it was true once. When I walked out I used to walk out with the thing. The formula. The client list. The recipe. Now the secret is on your servers and it updates without me. You don't need to stop me. You need to stop a data breach. That's a different clause.

---

[PM Burnham told the Innovation Nation Summit in Manchester](https://tech.eu/2026/10/09/uk-o-ban-non-compete-clauses-marking-boon-for-startup-and-scaleups/) that non-competes "will no longer be a barrier to hiring for our most promising start-ups and scaling firms." He called it the Bosman ruling for the innovation sector. He's right about the problem and wrong about what caused it.

The lobby — ElevenLabs, Synthesia, the Startup Coalition — points out that [about 5 million UK workers sit under non-compete contracts](https://tradersunion.com/news/financial-news/show/3731908-uk-tightens-non-compete-tech-finance/), and [87% of scale-up leaders say the clauses block hiring](https://tradersunion.com/news/financial-news/show/3731908-uk-tightens-non-compete-tech-finance/). Here's the number nobody published: 5 million multiplied by 87% is 4.35 million workers covered by a clause aimed at knowledge-in-skulls, in a sector where the most valuable IP now lives in weights-on-servers. Neither figure is interesting alone. Together they measure a mismatch.

The fairest objection: some AI knowledge still travels with the person. [Researchers departing Google DeepMind's London office have faced non-competes when launching rival AI startups](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/uk-tech-founders-urge-burnham-to-curb-non-competes-to-match-us-rivals/), and what they carry isn't lab notes — it's instinct about what's worth trying next. That's real. A blanket attack on non-competes doesn't resolve this; it removes the tool and leaves the problem.

[The CBI says it sees no evidence non-competes drag on innovation](https://tradersunion.com/news/financial-news/show/3731908-uk-tightens-non-compete-tech-finance/). Enterprise Britain says 87% of scale-ups disagree. When two named organisations are that far apart, the gap is the story, not either number.

Burnham's fix treats this as a competition problem. It's an IP problem with a new address. The question isn't whether the researcher can leave — it's what they're carrying when they go. Detail comes at the [Budget on 28 October](https://pathfounders.com/p/uk-to-ban-non-compete-clauses-after-tech-founders). That's when you find out if this is a scalpel or a sledgehammer.

The Bosman ruling freed footballers because the ball was theirs to kick wherever they chose. In AI, the ball is a two-hundred-billion-parameter model sitting on somebody else's servers. Nobody kicks it out the door when they quit.

The researcher has good instincts. The clause was written for a different kind of secret.
