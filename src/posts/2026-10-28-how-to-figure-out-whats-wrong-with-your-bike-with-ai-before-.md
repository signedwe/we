The bike has made that noise for six weeks. Not a crash noise. The other kind: a tick, a squeak, a skip that only happens in fourth gear going uphill. You've watched three YouTube videos. They're all for a slightly different version of the same problem on a completely different bike, and none of them mentions the click yours makes when you stand on the pedals.

At the end of this you'll have a ranked list of the most likely causes, the things to check first, and what to do about the common ones. A diagnosis shaped to what your bike is actually doing.

**What YouTube can't do**

A video gives the same answer regardless of who asked. Your phone's assistant asks follow-up questions. That back-and-forth is the whole job here.

**What you need**

Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini on your phone. Free tier works.

**Steps**

1. **Take two close-up photos.** The whole drivetrain from the right side, and whatever area seems worst: the chain, the derailleur, the brake caliper. Good light. Claude accepts image uploads in the mobile app and at claude.ai; ChatGPT and Gemini do the same. [Gear AI](https://www.gear-ai.app/) is a specialist app that identifies the part, rates the severity, and runs in voice mode with your hands on the components if you want that instead.

2. **Upload and describe the problem precisely.** "It clicks every pedal revolution on the right side, only under load, only since I rode in the rain" is useful. "It makes a weird noise" is not.

   *Try this:*

   *"I'm uploading two photos of my bike's drivetrain. It clicks every pedal revolution but only when I'm pushing hard, not freewheeling. Started after rain last week. Ask me whatever follow-up questions you need, then give me the most likely causes in order."*

3. **Answer the questions.** Which gears? Front or rear? How old is the chain? Did you check the pedals? Keep answering. This is how ten possible causes become two.

4. **Cross-check the fix on Park Tool.** Once you have a likely cause, go to [parktool.com](https://www.parktool.com) and search the specific adjustment: barrel adjuster, cable tension, crank bolt torque. Park Tool's repair content is free and professional. Use the AI for the diagnosis; use Park Tool for the wrench work.

5. **If you need a part name.** Photograph the component and ask what it's called and what to search to buy a replacement. Vision models read part names from photos better than most people read catalogues.

**What it costs**

Nothing.

**How long**

Ten minutes for the diagnosis. The fix depends on what it finds.

**Where it goes wrong**

The assistant can't hear your noise, only read your description of it. "A dry grinding that gets worse when it's cold" gives it more to work with than "a funny sound." It also can't feel play in a bearing or spot a hairline crack. In April 2026, Gary Marcus [documented cases](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/chatgpts-powerful-new-image-engine) where ChatGPT's vision made real mechanical errors on bike components, confusing brake positions and derailleur placement. Trust the conversation. Don't trust any diagram the assistant generates.

If the diagnosis points to wheel truing or a worn bottom bracket, those need a shop's tools.
