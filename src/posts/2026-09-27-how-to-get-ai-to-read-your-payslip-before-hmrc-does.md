Jamie glances at her payslip each month and thinks: looks about right. It probably isn't.

[A quarter of UK PAYE employees have received a wrong pay cheque](https://www.iris.co.uk/blog/payroll/reduce-payroll-errors-calculator/). Of those, 78% were underpaid. Multiply it out: roughly one in five workers is quietly losing money right now. A wrong tax code. A missed pension deduction. An emergency BR code that nobody cancelled after the new job started. [The BR code strips your entire personal allowance](https://www.payslipchecker.uk/check). Nobody sends a warning.

Here's what you'll have at the end: a plain verdict on your payslip and the words to use with payroll or HMRC if something's off. Fifteen minutes. No accountant.

**Step 1. Find your tax code first.** The code is upstream of every other number. [Log in to your HMRC Personal Tax Account at gov.uk/check-income-tax-current-year](https://www.gov.uk/check-income-tax-current-year). You need a Government Gateway login. It shows your code and what makes it up. [If the code has been wrong for years, you can claim back four tax years](https://taptax.co.uk/blog/hmrc-personal-tax-account-find-your-tax-code-fast). That's real money.

*Try this: paste your tax code and gross monthly pay into Claude or ChatGPT. Ask whether the PAYE on your payslip is correct for 2026/27. It'll do the arithmetic.*

**Step 2. Run your payslip through a checker.** Two free tools apply the live 2026/27 rules:

- [PayslipIQ](https://payslipiq.co.uk/check): upload a redacted photo or type the figures in. [Manual entry takes about 90 seconds](https://payslipiq.co.uk/). Covers tax, NI, pension and student loan. Cover your NI number before uploading.
- [PayslipChecker.uk](https://www.payslipchecker.uk/check): [your file never leaves your device](https://www.payslipchecker.uk/check). Nothing stored. Nothing sent.

Both are educational tools, not regulated advisers. They catch maths. They won't catch hours you didn't record.

**Step 3. If something's wrong, write before you speak.** Email payroll. Don't knock on a door. [If the error has run for months, your employer must correct the full amount owed](https://www.os-payroll.co.uk/post/payslip-errors-what-to-do). Not just this month's slice. Say that in the email.

**Step 4. Tax code wrong? Go to HMRC directly.** [Only HMRC can change your tax code. Your employer must use whatever code they're told to use](https://www.gov.uk/tax-codes). Contact them through the Personal Tax Account or call 0300 200 3300. [They usually fix it in two to four weeks](https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/paye/checking-your-tax-code/). The overpaid tax comes back through your next pay.

**Step 5. Still nothing?** [The Fair Work Agency can inspect payroll records going back six years](https://www.iris.co.uk/blog/payroll/reduce-payroll-errors-calculator/). It doesn't need a complaint from you to open a case. Report underpayment of the National Living Wage at [gov.uk/report-a-pay-issue](https://www.gov.uk/report-a-pay-issue). [The rate for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour from April 2026](https://www.theworkersrights.com/uk-payslip-audit-2026-check-underpayment/).

**Where it goes wrong.** These tools calculate on what you type. If your gross is wrong on the payslip, they won't know. They won't catch overtime arrears or salary sacrifice sitting outside standard fields. Check those against your contract.

**Who this replaces.** Payroll bureaux charge employers to answer exactly these questions. The worker never saw the invoice. They also never got the answer.
