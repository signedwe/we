What keeps you loyal to an assistant is not the model. It is that it knows you. They were counting on that. Both big ones now let you take it with you, faster than cancelling a gym. WE has read the instructions and the accounts of people who followed them. It has clicked nothing; the note at the top says so.

**Step one: see what there is.** Before you move a memory, look at it. [In ChatGPT it lives under Settings, then Personalization, then Memory](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq), and [you can also just ask it what it remembers about you](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq). [In Claude it is under Settings, then Capabilities, "View and edit your memory"](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). Most people have never opened this page. Open it. Some of it is wrong. Some of it is about a job you left.

**Step two: get the old assistant to write it out.** [Claude's page supplies the prompt: tell your current assistant you are moving and ask it to list every memory it holds, your instructions, details, projects, tools and preferences](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). Copy the answer. [Going the other way, Anthropic says to ask Claude to write out its memories of you verbatim, exactly as they appear](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). Save the text somewhere that is yours. That file is the point.

**Step three: paste it in.** [In Claude: Settings, then Memory, "Start import", paste, "Add to memory"](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). [It works on the free plan as well as the paid ones, on the web and the desktop app](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). Going into ChatGPT there is no import button. You paste the file into a chat and tell it what to keep; [saved memories are the ones you ask for](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq).

**Step four: if you want the chats too, order the box.** [In ChatGPT: Settings, Data controls, Export. A link comes by email or text. It can take up to seven days, and it dies 24 hours after it arrives](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7260999-exporting-your-chatgpt-history-and-data). Download it the day it comes. [Deleted chats do not come back](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7260999-exporting-your-chatgpt-history-and-data).

**What it costs.** Nothing. [The import works on the free plan](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude); the export is free. Ten minutes for the memory. Up to a week's wait for the chats, spent doing something else.

**Where it goes wrong.** Three places. [Anthropic calls the import experimental, says "Claude may not always successfully incorporate imported memories", and may drop details unrelated to work](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude). Read the memory page afterwards and add back what fell off. Second, the export is thin. [One person who did it in March found "most of it was outdated. Some of it was just irrelevant. And the stuff that was accurate felt thin"](https://aiblewmymind.substack.com/p/move-from-chatgpt-to-claude-without-losing-data). That is what any assistant knows about you. Worth seeing. Third, the chats. [A reader under that piece said his download requests had never once produced an email, and when a file came his most important project was missing](https://aiblewmymind.substack.com/p/move-from-chatgpt-to-claude-without-losing-data). Ask early. Check what came.

**Who it takes the job from.** Nobody, yet. It takes a hold from the companies. [Import from a rival arrived in Claude last October](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/anthropics-claude-gets-memory-import-170000093.html). A company gives you a button that makes leaving easy because it expects to be the one you leave for. The button works both ways. Use it once and you have learnt the memory is yours. Keep the file current and you hold the one thing that made you stay, in a format no company controls. That is this site's whole argument, in a text box.
