Someone in your family knows everything about everyone and has been waiting for years for someone to care enough to prove it.

Tonight you can make them a crossword. Not a generic one about capital cities. One where 14 Across is the name of the dog you had as kids, and 7 Down is the hotel your mum booked and immediately regretted, and the clue for MACCLESFIELD reads "Where Dad got lost on the way to a wedding (1994)."

A bespoke crossword from a professional constructor costs several hundred pounds. Writing the clues yourself takes longer than you'd think and mostly produces clues that are either obvious or baffling. AI writes crossword clues. That's the part nobody has tried yet with their own family.

**What you'll have at the end:** a printable crossword about your own family, with an answer key, ready in under an hour.

**What it costs:** nothing.

**Step 1.** Open Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Free tier is fine. Type this:

*I'm making a crossword puzzle about my family. Ask me questions one at a time: names, places, years, quirks, in-jokes, favourite things, memorable disasters. For each answer I give, write a crossword-style clue: indirect, not too easy, with the letter count in brackets. Thirty clues total.*

The assistant interviews you. Answer honestly. The holiday disaster, the car that kept breaking down, the phrase your dad repeats at every Christmas dinner. Clues come back in crossword format: "Two-word name of the cat who survived three house moves (4,4)." Spend twenty minutes here. The more specific your answers, the better the puzzle.

**Step 2.** Go to [101planners.com/crossword-puzzle-maker](https://www.101planners.com/crossword-puzzle-maker/). No account, no fee, nothing to install. [The site builds a finished puzzle from your own words](https://www.101planners.com/crossword-puzzle-maker/), assembles the grid automatically, numbers the clues, and generates a clean PDF with no watermark. Paste your words and clues in. Done.

**Step 3.** Print it. Hide the answer key page.

**Where it goes wrong.** The AI sometimes writes a clue so cryptic that only the person who gave the answer could solve it. Read every clue back as if you hadn't said it. Ask the AI to adjust individual ones. It will. [The grid tool at 101planners can struggle if too many of your words are long](https://www.101planners.com/crossword-puzzle-maker/). Mix lengths: a couple of long words, several short ones. If the grid won't settle, swap out the most awkward word and try again.
