*This consultation invites responses from anyone who was born.*

1. **Proposal.** From the start of the next financial year, all birthdays will take place on a single national date, 14 March.

2. **Background.** A Departmental review of one mid-sized office found birthdays occurring on as many as forty-one separate days a year. On each of these days, work stopped at 3.15pm so that eleven people could stand in a kitchen built for four and watch Martin look for a knife.

3. **Cake.** Under the current rota, Martin has brought the cake on every occasion since the rota was introduced. This includes his own birthday, which he added to the shared calendar himself under the title "Cake (mine)". Consolidation reduces Martin's commitment to one cake per year, to be shared nationally.

4. **Cards.** Officials traced one card that left Accounts on 2 May and reached its recipient on 23 May. By then it had been signed thirty-four times. Two people had signed it twice. One had written "Get well soon". The final signature belonged to a courier, whom Martin had asked if he wouldn't mind. Under consolidation, cards will no longer need to circulate, as everyone will be signing their own.

5. **Transitional arrangements for people already born.** Existing birthdays will be phased out over three years. In year one, each birthday will move halfway towards 14 March. Colleagues born in September will therefore celebrate in mid-December, where they will be absorbed into the Christmas party, as they always have been.

6. **Graham.** Graham in Facilities has told the office for eleven years that his birthday is 19 August, when two-thirds of staff are on leave and the remainder can be relied on not to sing. His actual birthday is 14 March. Graham is the only member of the public whose birthday does not move under these proposals. He has asked whether it could.

7. Graham's request for an exemption has been declined. Exemptions create overhead. He has since asked whether he could at least be on leave. Everyone will be on leave.

8. **Singing.** At 11am on 14 March the nation will sing the birthday song once, to everyone. The name will be sung as "dear all of us". Nobody will have to mouth the words while working out who the cake is for.

9. **Age.** Colleagues will no longer need to ask how old anyone is. Everyone will be one year older than they were, which officials have confirmed is already the case.

10. **Outstanding queries.** Martin has asked whether, now that everyone shares a birthday, someone will bring him a cake. His query has been referred to the cake rota.

11. **How to respond.** Please sign below and pass this paper to the next person. Martin will collect it in about three weeks.
