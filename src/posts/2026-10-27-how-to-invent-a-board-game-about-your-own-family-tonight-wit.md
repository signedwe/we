Your family has been playing the same four games since 1987. Somebody always cheats at Monopoly. Nobody has ever finished Catan. Tonight you can bin the lot and play something nobody has ever played before, about people you actually know.

Here's what you'll have at the end: a working board game. Custom rules. Cards that name real things your family does. A board you can draw on a piece of paper. Playable in under an hour. [AI did this for one person in an afternoon](https://medium.com/@piotrorzechowski/from-one-picture-to-a-board-game-how-ai-helped-me-build-a-game-in-one-afternoon-30c8bcb85c39), starting from a single photo.

**What you need.** Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT, free tier. Paper, pens, something to use as counters. Thirty minutes to design it.

**Step 1.** Open Claude or ChatGPT. Paste this, filling in the blanks:

*Design a simple board game for [number] players aged [ages]. The theme is [your family's obsession: cheese, football, arguing about directions, competitive baking]. Each player needs a goal, a way to move, and a way to block each other. Give me the rules in under 200 words, a list of 10 event cards, and instructions for drawing the board on A4 paper.*

The more specific you are, the stranger and better the result. [ChatGPT and Claude both handle this well](https://shufflekit.com/blog/best-ai-board-game-makers). Generic inputs get generic games.

**Step 2.** Read the rules back aloud. Something will be broken. Ask the AI to fix it: *Player two has no way to catch up if they fall behind. Add a mechanic for that.* Keep going until a seven-year-old could follow it.

**Step 3.** Ask for the event cards in full. Something like:

*Write all 10 event cards as single sentences. Each one should cause mild chaos. Name them after things this family actually does.*

Then write them on scraps of paper and shuffle them.

**Step 4.** Draw the board. The AI will give you a description. A path of squares works. A grid works. Anything works as long as everyone can see it.

**Step 5.** Play it. It will be unbalanced. That is the point. Argue about the rules. Ask the AI to referee mid-game if you genuinely cannot agree. [It can adjudicate](https://www.riis.com/blog/creating-board-games-using-chatgpt).

**What it costs.** Nothing. Free tier on both platforms.

**How long.** Thirty minutes to build. An hour to play, longer if someone takes the event cards personally.

**Where it goes wrong.** [The AI is unreliable on probability and balance](https://shufflekit.com/blog/best-ai-board-game-makers). Do not trust it to tell you the game is fair. Playtest it for five minutes before you commit. It also defaults to safe, familiar mechanics if you give it nothing to work with. Push back. Ask for something weirder.

**Why not just buy one?** You could. But bought games are about strangers. This one is about whoever is in the room tonight.
