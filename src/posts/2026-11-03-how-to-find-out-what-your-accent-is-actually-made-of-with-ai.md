Your family says you've gone posh since you moved. Your flatmate says you've gone northern. You have no idea who's right.

Twenty minutes. That's all it takes to settle it. You'll end up with a phonetic breakdown of your actual vowels, which regional varieties are in there, and an audio file of yourself sounding like someone who grew up three hundred miles away.

**What you need:** your phone, the free [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com) (or gemini.google.com in a browser), and a free account at [twoshot.app](https://twoshot.app/ai-accent-changer/). Nothing else.

**Step 1. Record sixty seconds of yourself talking.** Don't read from a text. That stiffens everything. Tell your phone what you did at the weekend. Your voice memo app is fine. Save it as an M4A or MP3.

**Step 2. Upload it to Gemini.** [Gemini's free tier accepts up to ten minutes of audio per prompt](https://screenapp.io/features/audio-ask-ai). Attach your recording. Paste this:

*'Analyse the accent in this recording. Name the regional English variety. Describe the specific vowel sounds: how the TRAP, BATH and STRUT vowels sound, whether there's rhoticity, how the GOAT vowel lands. Say which influences are present and how strong the accent is overall.'*

Gemini gives you a phonetic breakdown of your actual speech. Not the accent you imagine you have. The one on the recording.

**Step 3. Go to [twoshot.app](https://twoshot.app/ai-accent-changer/).** Upload the same recording. Pick a target accent. Received Pronunciation, Geordie, West Country, General American, Scottish Standard English. [The tool separates your vocal identity from your accent features](https://twoshot.app/ai-accent-changer/). Your timbre stays. The vowels shift. Download what comes back.

**What it costs:** nothing. Both tools are free at the level this needs.

**How long:** twenty minutes.

**Where it goes wrong:** Gemini works best on clear audio with no background noise. Go somewhere quiet. Under thirty seconds and the analysis gets thin. [TwoShot supports over twenty regional English accents](https://twoshot.app/ai-accent-changer/), but the result is rougher on very strong regional speech. It's a re-synthesis, not a performance. It won't sound exactly like a native speaker. It sounds like you, if you'd grown up somewhere else.

The bit nobody mentions: Gemini often tells you your accent is a mix of two regions. Or that you've kept one feature from somewhere you left a decade ago. The BATH vowel is the usual tell for people who moved between north and south. You might find out you're still more Midlands than you thought.

*A note from a speech and language therapist:*

Accent isn't fixed. It shifts across contexts. You sound different with strangers than at home. One sixty-second recording captures one register, one moment. The BATH vowel you use telling a story to your phone may not be the one you use in a job interview. Gemini will give you the accent on that recording. That's real. It's also incomplete. One more thing: for people with a stammer or a speech sound difference, hearing an AI version of your own voice in a different accent can be strange. I'd want that flagged.
