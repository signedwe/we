Sarah's handwriting is terrible and she knows it. But it's *hers*. The capital S that leans left. The y with the extra curl. Turning that into something you could type with used to mean hiring a typographer. [The whole process now runs free in a browser](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html), in about half an hour, and your handwriting never leaves your device.

**What you'll have at the end:** a real, installable font built from your own letterforms. Use it in Word, on birthday cards, on jam jar labels, anywhere a font goes.

**Why not just YouTube?** A video teaches you someone else's process. [FontCrafter](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html) looks at *your* handwriting, traces *your* letterforms, and builds a file no other person on earth owns. A video cannot do that.

---

**What you need:** a dark felt-tip pen. [Ballpoints are too faint; thick markers bleed](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html). White paper. A phone or scanner. A desktop browser for the last step.

**Step 1.** Go to [FontCrafter](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html) and download the template PDF. Print it at 100%, not 'fit to page'. It's a grid of labelled boxes, one for each letter, number and punctuation mark.

**Step 2.** Fill every box in your own hand. Write naturally. [The tool captures up to three variants of each character](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html) and cycles between them as you type. Repeated letters don't look like rubber stamps. The quirks are the point.

**Step 3.** Photograph the filled sheet flat on a table in even daylight. [Shadows across the page get read as ink and warp the letters](https://androxus.com/blogs/add-your-own-handwriting-font). A sharp phone photo in good light works fine.

**Step 4.** Drop the photo back into [FontCrafter](https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html). Everything runs in your browser. No account. No upload. The tool traces your letterforms and exports a working .otf or .ttf file. Download it.

**Step 5.** Install it. On Windows: right-click, Install. On Mac: double-click, then Install Font. Your handwriting now lives in Word, Pages, Canva, every card app.

**Step 6, which is the good bit.** Go to [PenSend](https://arcade.pirillo.com/pensend.html), load your font, type a message, and export it as a PNG. Send it. The recipient will think you wrote it.

*Tip from [the Hacker News thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47306655): if detection fails, try reprinting the template at exactly 100% so all four corner marks are on the sheet.*

---

**How long:** 30 to 45 minutes.

**Costs:** nothing.

**Where it goes wrong:** ballpoints produce broken outlines. Shadows distort letters. Joined cursive doesn't convert well. Each character needs to sit as a separate stroke inside its box. Print lettering only.
