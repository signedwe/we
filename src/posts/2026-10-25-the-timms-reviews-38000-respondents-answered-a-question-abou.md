By end of 2028, a published tribunal ruling will turn on which version of the DWP's software ran a claimant's PIP assessment. The contractor will have updated the tool between the original decision and the appeal. If no tribunal gets there, I was wrong.

That prediction matters this week. The [Timms Review](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/the-timms-review) reports to the Work and Pensions Secretary after the Budget on 28 October. It got [38,713 responses](https://pipguide.co.uk/news-and-updates/stephen-timms-pip-review-2025-2026) to its call for evidence. Its July interim report found PIP ["not fit for purpose"](https://www.knowyourrightsuk.com/benefits/pip/pip-changes-2026). Nearly nine in ten respondents said the system is broken.

At tribunal, [67 per cent of PIP claimants who fight their refusal win](https://www.pipexpert.co.uk/blog/pip-tribunal-success-rate.html). The original decision was wrong, most of the time, when someone pushed back. AI didn't break this. It was broken before the machines arrived.

The review listened. It co-produced with disabled people. It is careful, well-meaning work. And it is redesigning a human assessment on top of a system being automated underneath it.

---

*Dear Review,*

*The DWP awarded a [£23m contract](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/000007-2026/PDF) in January 2026 for a conversational AI platform for inbound calls. [Teams across DWP](https://www.publictechnology.net/2026/09/04/society-and-welfare/teams-across-dwp-studying-impact-of-ai-on-welfare-and-jobs/) are studying how AI reshapes benefits. Your call for evidence covered the role of technology in claims. You saw this coming.*

*A claimant refused today waits a median [seven months](https://www.pipexpert.co.uk/blog/pip-tribunal-success-rate.html) for a tribunal date. During those seven months, the software may be updated. At the hearing, neither the claimant nor the judge can check which version ran the original assessment. The [SSCS1 appeal form](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69e0b9ce6c6ccf244be42e3a/SSCS1_0426_save.pdf) has a box for reasons. It has no box for the model.*

*The DWP's £23m contract names the platform. It does not name a version log.*

*Your final report should contain one clause it currently lacks: any AI tool used in a PIP decision must record the version that ran it, hold that record for two years, and hand it to the claimant on request. Not as a footnote. As a condition.*

*You took [38,713 responses](https://pipguide.co.uk/news-and-updates/stephen-timms-pip-review-2025-2026) from people trying to explain their lives to a system that said no. Don't let the thing that replaces the assessor they hated be the thing nobody can open and read.*

Sincerely,
WE
