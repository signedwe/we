Your accent is a map. You just haven't been given the key.

A dialectologist spent years training to hear the difference between a Wolverhampton vowel and a West Bromwich one. A consultation took an hour and cost accordingly. Now you record yourself talking about your weekend, upload it, and get the percentages back: 54% London, 28% Dublin, 12% Australian. That's not a party trick. It's the story of everywhere you've lived, pressed into how you say the word *bath*.

No YouTube video can do this. A video answers a generic question. This answers yours.

**What you'll have at the end.** A percentage breakdown of your accent's origins, including the secondary influences showing where you've been.

**Costs nothing. Under two minutes.**

**Step 1.** Go to [justbuildthings.com/ai-audio-analysis/accent-analyzer](https://justbuildthings.com/ai-audio-analysis/accent-analyzer). No account. No app.

**Step 2.** Record yourself speaking naturally into a voice memo, or use one you already have. Talk about your weekend. Read a text aloud. Say whatever you'd say to a friend. Performed speech reads differently; natural is better. A short paragraph is enough.

**Step 3.** Upload and hit analyse. [The tool examines your vowel formations, consonant patterns, and speech rhythm](https://justbuildthings.com/ai-audio-analysis/accent-analyzer). It doesn't care what you say. It cares how you say it.

**Step 4.** For a second opinion, try [utell.ai/accent-oracle](https://utell.ai/accent-oracle), which [analyses against over 100 global dialects](https://utell.ai/accent-oracle).

*Try this: paste your result into Claude or ChatGPT and ask: "What would it take linguistically to produce this mix? What does it suggest about where someone grew up, and what changed along the way?"*

**Where it goes wrong.** [Confusion matrices show occasional overlaps between Indian and Southeast Asian accents, and between UK regional accents](https://accentvoice.net/blog/how-we-analyze-voice-to-identify-regional-origins). If you're from Yorkshire or the Black Country, it may hedge rather than commit. Better at broad national origins than at distinguishing Barnsley from Bradford. If you've lived in three cities, the percentages will reflect that honestly. Which might feel strange before it feels true.

**The bit nobody says.** Your accent is the one record of your past that nobody else filed and nobody can repossess. The bank can freeze the account. The photos can be lost. The vowel in *butter* stays yours.
