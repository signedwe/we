**APOLOGISER (part-time).** Large energy supplier seeks human to say sorry. Must sound tired. No authority to fix anything; that is handled elsewhere, by nobody. Own headset an advantage. Previous applicants need not reapologise.

**WITNESS.** Couple, both busy, require a person to be present at dinner on Thursdays so that it counts. No conversation needed. Must be able to say "that looks nice" unprompted. Dessert provided.

**HUMAN IN THE LOOP.** You will sit in the loop. 37.5 hours a week. The loop is in Swindon. Please do not touch anything in the loop.

**SECOND OPINION.** Board of medium-sized firm needs someone to disagree with the machine once a quarter, for the minutes. You will be overruled. Lunch included. Strong views not essential and, on reflection, not wanted.

**QUEUE.** National museum of customer service seeks twelve people to stand in a line. Authentic sighing a plus. Ideal for those who remember.

**HANDWRITER.** Must be able to write "thinking of you" as if a person meant it. Left-handers preferred for the smudge. Volume: 4,000 cards a day. You will not be told who died.

**PERSON TO BLAME (permanent).** Regulated firm requires named individual. Duties: to have been responsible. Salary generous; pension, realistically, unlikely to be needed. Would suit someone with no dependants and a calm face.

**GRANDSON, RELIEF.** Sundays, 2 till 4. Must not check phone, own one or resemble one. Knowledge of the 1980s helpful. She will call you Martin. Let her.

**ARTIST'S STRUGGLE (consultant).** Successful image generator seeks human to have suffered on its behalf for press purposes. Garret supplied. Must provide own childhood.

**MEETING.** We are looking for six to eight people to be a meeting. No agenda. Biscuits. One of you will be asked to take an action away and never mention it again. Hybrid: two of you will be on a screen, frozen.

**SOMEONE.** Just someone. Tuesdays. Ring the bell.
