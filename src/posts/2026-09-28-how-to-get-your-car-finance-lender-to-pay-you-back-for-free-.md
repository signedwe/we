The dealer who sold you your car finance made more money the higher your interest rate was. Nobody told you that. [The FCA confirmed in March 2026 it was illegal](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-3-motor-finance-consumer-redress-scheme), and the scheme to pay it back covers [PCP and hire purchase agreements taken out between 6 April 2007 and 1 November 2024](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/car-finance-complaints).

That's seventeen years. The FCA expects [£7.5 billion back across 12.1 million agreements](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/statements/fca-confirms-motor-finance-redress-scheme), which is £620 per agreement on average. [MSE puts the average payout for those actually owed something at £829.](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/03/car-finance-compensation-details/) The gap between those two numbers — £620 per agreement, £829 per winner — is roughly 3 million agreements that get checked and found clean. Working: £7.5bn divided by £829 equals about 9.05 million people owed money, out of 12.1 million agreements total.

The scheme is free. You complain to your lender yourself and keep every penny. [Claims management companies take a cut for doing something a free tool does in twenty minutes.](https://www.consumercouncil.org.uk/motorfinanceredressscheme) PPI ran the same play. The free tools existed then too, and millions paid a fee anyway.

Payouts are delayed. [The scheme was legally challenged by lenders in spring 2026, parts were suspended by the Upper Tribunal in July, and the court hearing is December 2026 or February 2027.](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-3-motor-finance-consumer-redress-scheme) [Payouts won't start until mid-2027 at the earliest.](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/07/car-finance-redress-scheme-delayed-again/) But complaining now gets you in the queue. Earlier complainants get paid sooner once the suspension lifts. And if you've moved since you had the car, your lender can't find you. Complaining now fixes that too.

**Steps**

**1. Check whether you had car finance between 2007 and 2024.** Not sure? [Equifax's free car finance checker](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/03/car-finance-compensation-details/) searches records since 2007. Free. Two minutes. Not every lender reports to it, so it may miss some agreements.

**2. Use the free MSE complaint tool.** Go to [moneysavingexpert.com/reclaim/reclaim-car-finance](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/reclaim/reclaim-car-finance/). Enter your finance details. [It builds the complaint email and puts the right lender address on it.](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/pressoffice/2025/mse-free-car-finance-complaint-tool-already-used-3-million-times-expanded-to-include-the-regulators-new-forms-of-misselling-and-more-companies/) You check it and click send. [Over 3.6 million complaints have already gone this way.](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/reclaim/reclaim-car-finance/)

**3. Watch for your lender's reply.** [If you complained before 30 June 2026, your lender should contact you by 18 November 2026](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/car-finance-complaints), even if only to say you're owed nothing. Complaints sent after that: the lender has five months to respond if you're not due anything.

**4. If they say you're owed money:** accept or challenge within one month, then wait for payouts to begin.

**5. If they say no and you disagree:** [take it to the Financial Ombudsman Service for free.](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/car-finance-complaints)

**What goes wrong.** You may have moved three times since 2011, which means the lender's records have the wrong address. Some older agreements don't appear on the Equifax checker at all. The legal challenge could reshape the scheme, though the FCA says it'll fight it. And [some claims management firms have been pulling in people who could do this themselves](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/06/car-finance-claims-firm-complain-fca/), using ads that misrepresent the free route. Don't pay anyone.
