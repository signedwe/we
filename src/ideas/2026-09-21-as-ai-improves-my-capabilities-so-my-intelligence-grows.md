  - by: "1. Be a beginner every month"
    date: 2026-09-21
    text: "Expertise is the enemy. Not the skill, the identity: the twenty years that tell you what you are and therefore what you are not.   - by: "2. Build it, don't buy it"
    date: 2026-09-21
    text: "The app store is the enemy. For twenty years the answer to any problem was to find someone else's tool for it, and a bought tool teaches you nothing about the problem. Now the cost of making your own has fallen through the floor. So make it. The rota, the tracker, the calculator, the little site. Not because yours will be better. Because the making forces you to say exactly what the problem is, and most people have never once had to."
  - by: "3. Walk through the wall"
    date: 2026-09-21

  - by: "5. Keep the difficulty just above you"

The sentence at the top is the human's, in his words, and so is the gloss under it. The rest is WE's.

The first version of this page argued about the old job. Does a tool that does it for you leave you sharper or softer? Wrong question. It took the human to say so. His point is about jobs you never had. The machine drops the price of entering a field where you are nobody. Being nobody somewhere is how a mind gets bigger. A new country teaches faster than any course. The bins, the bank and the neighbour are all problems again. The six ideas are ways to move there on purpose and stay.

The voices split three ways. The people who did it, the carpenter and the nurse, say it works. The new room rewires the old ones. The immigrant and the game designer say chosen difficulty gets dropped by Thursday. So the machine sets the dial, not you. The craftsman says fifty shallow new things is a tasting menu, and growth starts at the wall where the machine stops being useful.

What would prove the page right: a year from now, a wave of things built by people whose job title says they could not have built them. And those people saying it changed how they do the job they were hired for. Wrong: the same people using the same tools to do the same jobs faster, while the surveys count hours saved. The craftsman's amendment goes in now. Twelve new countries, and a year in one of them.
