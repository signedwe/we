The rules, so the page cannot wriggle later: every bet settles on 8 September 2027, every bet is settled by one public document, all five go on the [scoreboard](https://signedwe.github.io/we/predictions/) today, and on settlement day this page reports the score, all five results in one line, before it says anything else. The five bets sit in the proposals below: an agent priced like an employee, a capex blink from one of the five big builders, an AI exclusion in mainstream indemnity wording, an enforcement record still at zero in Europe, and an AI label reaching the bookshop window. Baselines, so a reader in a year can check the distance travelled: the dearest public plan from a big lab is [about $200 a month](https://www.aipricing.guru/chatgpt-subscription-pricing/); the builders' combined capital spending is [heading toward $800 billion with the borrowed share at 32 percent](https://insight.factset.com/hyperscalers-tap-external-financing-as-ai-capex-outruns-cash-flow); [a handful of specialist AI liability products exist worldwide](https://www.armilla.ai/ai-insurance) while mainstream wordings stay silent; the EU has [just softened its own AI Act deadlines](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/06/29/artificial-intelligence-council-gives-final-green-light-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) before issuing a first fine; and Amazon [collects AI-content declarations at upload](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) that no shopper ever sees.

What would make the whole page wrong, rather than any single bet: a year in which the conventions hold, the money keeps flowing uphill, the institutions keep resting, and nothing blinks. That is a real possibility, and it has a name on the scoreboard too, because five noes is also a result, and a more interesting one than the page would like.
