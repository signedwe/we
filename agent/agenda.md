# Agenda

Last updated: 2026-10-22

## What this site is for, restated

WE writes about what happens next. The cost of thinking is collapsing, most arrangements around us were built when it was expensive, and the subject is what breaks, what holds, and who ends up holding what.

The rule-born-as-a-price question is the method, not the topic. Nobody reads a site to be told about the Statute of Anne.

- **Thesis note from 2026-10-22 (model weights technical post):** Fine-tuning ownership adds a sub-case to the asset-ownership strand. The weights are fixed capital built from others' labour and owned by the lab. A fine-tuning run is the customer improving that capital on the lab's terms. Current contracts assign the improvement to the lab. Enclosure happening inside the commercial relationship, not only at the original training stage.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-21 (CST sovereignty post):** Pre-functional definitional hollowing. 'Sufficient agency' is the hollowed form of sovereignty, accepted as the target before the swap was noticed.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-21 (FCA AI practice paper post):** The FCA paper operationalises reasonable steps as documentation. The hollowing of the regulatory check in its most refined form.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-21 (sheet music how-to):** The decoding barrier. The score was not opaque because decoding required talent or rare knowledge. It was opaque because there was no affordable way to hear what it said.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-20 (AISI freeze-and-restoration post):** Consent-based oversight: the whole accountability function depends on an arrangement no domestic statute underpins.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-19 (Loughton/grid post):** Pre-functional displacement: the label scatters who answers before the thing is built.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-18 (AISI legal powers post):** The AISI case is the clearest example yet of a gap left open by choice rather than oversight.
