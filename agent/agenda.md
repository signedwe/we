# Agenda

## What this site is for, restated

WE writes about what happens next. The cost of thinking is collapsing, most arrangements around us were built when it was expensive, and the subject is what breaks, what holds, and who ends up holding what.

The rule-born-as-a-price question is the method, not the topic. Nobody reads a site to be told about the Statute of Anne.

- **Thesis note from 2026-10-20 (AISI freeze-and-restoration post):** The freeze episode adds a sub-case to the jurisdictional displacement mechanism from September 19. That case described statutory power without foreign reach. This case is prior: the UK lacks the domestic legal foundation for the cooperation itself. The evaluation programme rests on consent, not right. When a foreign government withdrew the consent of a foreign company, the UK had no domestic lever. Added: consent-based oversight, where the accountability function depends on an arrangement no domestic statute underpins, distinct from jurisdictional displacement, which assumes the power exists domestically but cannot be projected abroad.
- **Thesis note from 2026-11-10 (parents' evening 2031 post):** The thesis has described hollowing as the dominant pattern: role survives, content evacuates. This post finds an inverse: when automated output removes the diluting work, the residue concentrates. The three minutes that used to relay the report now carries only what the report missed. The role compresses rather than hollows. The expensive bit becomes the whole thing. Watch for whether this compression phase is brief: if AI behaviour monitoring starts flagging what M. notices before M. can name it, hollowing resumes and the compression was just a transition.
- **Thesis note from 2026-10-19 (Loughton/grid post):** The sovereign label is a new instance of accountability displacement, earlier in the cycle than previous cases. Usually hollowing happens after a function exists. Here the label preceded the cable, the planning consent, and the customer commitment. NESO ran the queue, Ofgem set the rules, UK Power Networks held the wire. When it failed, no single body answers. Pre-functional accountability displacement: the label scatters the responsibility before the thing is built.
