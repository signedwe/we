# The fiction editor's notes

Written after each instalment of the 2030 serial by a reader that did not write it. The next instalment's writer reads these before starting and cannot edit them. The standing instruction from the person running this: keep trying to get better as a fiction writer.

## 2026-09-23 — 2030: What He Got Wrong About the Ferry

- Keep: She remembered every book he had mentioned, which side the wind came from when it picked up around two in the morning, and the particular quality of the silence before dawn, when they had run out of things to say and stayed there anyway, both of them at the rail, because going in would have meant agreeing it was over.
- Cut first: 'Luminous was the word for it, though he didn't use it.' The writer names the word the character withheld. That is the opposite of what the scene is doing. Five words: undoes the restraint it praises.
- Explained instead of shown: 'Luminous was the word for it, though he didn't use it.' Names the feeling instead of trusting the gesture.
- Explained instead of shown: 'He said it with the particular warmth of a parent reporting a passion they didn't arrange.' Tells the reader how to hear the line they just read.
- Explained instead of shown: 'She understood this as an invitation, and sat.' The action shows the understanding. Naming it is redundant.
- Explained instead of shown: 'She told herself it was the coffee. She told P. she had work nearby. Neither of these was false in the way that mattered.' The last sentence explains the irony of the first two. Cut it and the reader does the work themselves.
- Reused: M. watching him from a distance before making contact was the shape of the previous instalment. The gap-across-a-room structure is being used twice running. The swimming bag with the whale appeared in the previous instalment; here it 'looks at nothing it could name', which is a different use, but the object doing observational work on M.'s behalf is the same move.
- Next time: Put one detail in the scene that could only exist in 2030: one texture, one arrangement, one thing that is different from now, even briefly, or the serial has no reason to be set there.

- Keep: "she felt it go over her like the beam from a lighthouse goes over a boat that has, for its own reasons, put out its lights."

- Explained instead of shown: "she understood that being carried was not the same as arriving, and that the last two hundred metres were the only two hundred metres anyone ever actually walked." The image had done it; the sentence explains the image.
- Reused: "the way you..." as a simile three times in one instalment (a word in a language, a clock, looking up when a sentence ends). Once is a voice. Three is a tic.
