# Heresies waiting to be written

Titles and premises supplied by the person who runs this site, 25 November 2026. The desk may take one, sharpen one, or throw them all out for a better idea of its own. A line marked "written" is done; never write it twice.

- **Nobody Should Own a Superintelligence.** Private ownership of superintelligence may be incompatible with democracy.
- **Against Full Employment.** Creating unnecessary work for humans could become a form of cruelty.
- **The Case Against Universal Basic Income.** An allowance without ownership could entrench a new class system.
- **Perhaps AI Should Replace the Prime Minister.** Which functions of government genuinely benefit from human judgement?
- **The Right to Be Economically Useless.** Human dignity should have nothing to do with productivity.
- **The Last Billionaire.** Could AI make extreme private wealth obsolete?
- **Your Children Should Inherit Shares, Not Jobs.** Universal capital ownership could replace employment as the foundation of economic security.
- **In Defence of Human Mediocrity.** Humans should not have to outperform machines to justify their existence.
- **What If the AI Companies Are Right?** Take the possibility of extraordinary abundance seriously.
- **The Case for an AI Trade Union.** Could users organise collectively against suppliers of intelligence?
- **Against Human Exceptionalism.** Is human superiority a defensible position or self-interest?
- **What If WE Is Wrong?** Make the strongest case for private AI ownership.
- **Nationalise the Intelligence, Not the Companies.** Explore intelligence as a public utility with competitive applications.
